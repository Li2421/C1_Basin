# Pilot timing case-selection deviation

The Stage A preregistration described the approximately 100 timing cases as planned **train** cases. The new `run.py` initially selected 98 equally spaced cases from the whole frozen 86,016-case list, including train and test cases, spanning all seven controllers. This discrepancy was found while the timing pilot was running. The process was not used to inspect success outcomes or to choose states, η, controllers, models, or endpoints; only `elapsed_seconds` will be used for the fixed 22-CPU cost calculation. All pilot raw and journal records remain preserved and count toward the final requested panel if the 64-state design remains. The exact 98 rollout UIDs are in `PILOT_CASE_UIDS.json`. The timing sample is arguably more representative of the whole panel, but that does not erase the preregistration deviation. No outcome-dependent decision is permitted from these records.
