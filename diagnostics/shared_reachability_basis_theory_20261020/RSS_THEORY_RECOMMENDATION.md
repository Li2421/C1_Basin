# RSS theory framing and evidence boundary

## Recommended main contribution

State a **local shared safe coordination structure**: for fixed task/geometry/dynamics/safety, a nonzero-volume Toy conflict class admits an explicit full-CBF, finite-horizon reference escape independent of nominal controller. Derive the topological relative-separation need at longitudinal-order reversal and use stage demands as transparent, sufficient progress tests. Then state an **exact box-feasibility theorem** for the original `G,L,R` correction and a **conditional TT exploitability theorem** through exact Euclidean projection. The theorem is useful because its checks involve three gains, finite stage rows, reference margins, and frozen actions; it does not assume a controller already escapes.

Precise suggested phrase: **“The original three-column correction is a requirement-informed, low-dimensional family with certified positive-margin raw coverage on a nontrivial local safe-passing domain and conditional, locally robust TT realization. Coverage across a broad controller/state population remains an empirical question.”** Avoid “covers most safe escape motions,” “shared deadlock trajectory,” or “one η works for all controllers.” The Toy reference proof and TT certificate are connected by checkable conditions; the present archive has not certified those conditions across the Toy reference domain.

## Main text versus appendix

Main text: reach-avoid object and why controller independence is structural; nondegenerate local existence proposition; necessary relative-order/separation argument; exact original basis formulas; the raw `m*` box criterion; TT stage intersection and adaptive reference-distance sufficient condition; concise counterexample at pair contact; explicit statement that local action progress is weaker than closed-loop escape.

Appendix: eight Toy finite wall segments, all CBF row calculations, the `E→K_entry→K_pass` 798-step budget, geometric volume denominators, the regularized Perp cancellation proof, LP/minimax derivation, numeric source/checkpoint manifest, archived action witness and solver tolerances, and the conditional continuity lemma. Ring's old symmetric rotation construction belongs at most in a cautionary appendix because its goal-radius class is absent from the audited benchmark; do not search for another Ring template in this theory paper step.

## Claims to avoid or test later

Do not posit globally inactive walls, universal active-set stability, a uniform bound on arbitrary controller outputs, global correction cone coverage, monotone goal progress, or one-step-to-task-success equivalence. These make the theorem appear strong by encoding the desired result as an assumption. The original box excludes `η_G=0`; any no-Goal ablation is diagnostic and outside the policy family. Perp can vanish with the safe anchor, and many-agent Relative may cancel.

A claim about “large fraction” first needs a declared state domain or sampling distribution, controller population/key protocol, stage action rule fixed independently of success, and unit of inference. Then reconstruct exact anchors/B at saved states with replay verification, evaluate the continuous `Θ` or explicitly call a menu estimate a lower bound, and report controller/state-clustered intervals. A matched Toy audit should include eligible `K_entry/K_pass` states; the existing 68-action panel has none. A Ring audit would need a stage description corresponding to observed coordination and exact TT source reconstruction; the existing narrow template is excluded, and archived broader-candidate actions do not contain the precorrection anchor/B. These are future evidence needs, not authorization for new inference or rollout in this task.

Third-layer state/controller conditioning remains an interface: a selector may choose `η(x,c,ξ)` among feasible gains, but no theorem here gives transfer to unseen controllers or states. Report independent learning outcomes only under their original protocols. The strongest present RSS claim is **existence + requirement-aware expressive capacity + conditional TT realization**, with an explicit measured mechanism gap. It is stronger than the tautology that `R_safe` is controller independent and narrower than a universal shared escape policy.
