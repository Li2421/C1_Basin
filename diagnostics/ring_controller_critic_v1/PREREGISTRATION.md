## Evidence and population

Use Round15's 57,344 rows (56 controllers, 14 recipes, 32 states, 8 schedule arms, four future seeds), frozen R5 and R6 arrays, and historical physical23. Do not generate rollouts or probes. Round5/6 K16 fixed-eta labels have different execution semantics; reuse their bounded-residual design, not their labels. ## Features and model

Arm order: F10,F13,F2,S200,S300,S400,S500,S600. For every state, compute round6 physical23 via `controller_q_transfer_round3_v1/state_generator.py` and its historical normalization file; then z-score each of 23 features using training-controller states only, replacing zero standard deviations by 1. PCA2: R5 columns 105:110 are excluded per round15; remaining columns are imputed with their training-column finite means, standardized on training controllers, then SVD PCA with two components; all-missing columns are removed, signs made deterministic by positive largest-magnitude loading. R6-Δ is the seven-column `delta` array; standardize from training controllers only, replacing non-finite values with training-column median and zero scales by 1. No external feature contributes to normalization or PCA.

A0 is physical23 + eight-arm one-hot through 64/64/16 SiLU hidden layers and one scalar logit. A1 is frozen matching-seed A0 logit plus a 16/16/16 SiLU residual on [physical23,R5-PCA2,one-hot], with zero-initialized output and 1.0·tanh bound. A2 is the same with seven R6-Δ inputs appended. A3 is a fresh 64/64/16 SiLU scalar-logit network on [physical23,R5-PCA2,one-hot]. All outputs use sigmoid for probability. A0/A3 AdamW weight decay 0.0001; A1/A2 0.01. Gradient global norm clip 5. JAX x64 false, FP32, no mixed precision.

## Fitting and source validation

Sort 14 recipe names lexically; fold k=0..6 holds out recipes k and k+7. Fit each model with seeds 17,23,41 and AdamW lr 0.0008 for 12,000 updates, minibatch 128 valid controller×state×arm cells sampled uniformly with replacement; use a cell's four-seed success count / valid count as target and weight its BCE by valid count / 4. Unknown outcomes are excluded. Checkpoints at steps 500,1000,...,12000. For each model, choose a single step minimizing mean held-recipe NLL over all seven folds and three seeds; tie goes to earlier step. This is an internal source validation choice. CV metrics are DEVELOPMENT and selected on the same folds, so may be optimistic. Freeze selected steps, then refit all models from scratch on all 56 controllers at their selected step with the same three seeds. In each fold and full refit, A1/A2 base is the matching A0 seed checkpoint trained on that same source partition.

Save all three fit checkpoints, source-held predictions, and T1/T2/T3 predictions for each model where features are available. Predictions are for every controller×state×eight-arm pair before masking test menus; A2 external is unavailable. Freeze code, selected steps, model checkpoints, feature transforms, predictions, rules, and input identity hashes in `MODEL_FREEZE.receipt.json` before opening any T1–T3 success/unknown values.

## Selection and scores

Eligible arms are training menu ∩ labeled external menu, with Freg excluded from selection and retained for oracle. All argmax ties choose first in the fixed arm order. Ensemble probability is the mean of three fit probabilities. Primary state rule selects its per-state maximum. Pessimistic rule selects mean minus population SD across three fit probabilities (κ=1). Controller-fixed rule first averages ensemble probability across all states, then picks one arm. Source-mean rule selects the arm with highest mean success among training labels over all source controllers/states/seeds, restricted to eligible arms; in CV use only that fold's source recipes. G_E uses round15 source-label E=S500−F10 at the controller level, R5-PCA2 and ridge penalty 1 with centered X and y, as in round15. In each CV fold, fit only source recipes; for external, fit all 56 source controllers. The source-trained Ê model and all external G_E choices are frozen with critic predictions before opening external labels. Ê<0.05 chooses F10, otherwise S500. F10 is constant. Oracle is best labeled action per controller among all labeled arms, using lower-bound success; it is a hindsight reference, not a selectable policy.

Primary outcome treats unknown as failure. Sensitivity treats unknown as success. Average seed outcome per controller×state×arm. A policy's value is mean across states and controllers. Controller regret is its oracle mean success minus its policy mean success; report average and maximum controller regret in percentage points. In CV oracle and eligible menu are all eight arms. Model NLL/Brier use known labels only, over every eligible action and all controllers/states/seeds. Calibration: ten equal-width probability bins with predicted mean, observed known-label frequency and count. Inferred E is predicted S500−F10 mean across states; inferred L is predicted S500−F13 mean across states, matching round15 definitions, reported as descriptive correlations with matching observed differences.

On T1, Q1 compares A1-state vs A0-state paired mean regrets, Q2 compares A1-state regret with G_E plus 0.5pp and requires A1 max controller regret ≤5pp, Q3 compares A1-state vs A1-controller-fixed paired regrets. For Q1/Q3, draw 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples of the 64 state indices, shared across controllers, arms, and policies, seed 1616; compute percentile 95% CI on difference. Strict upper bound <0 passes. Q2 is a deterministic point-estimate gate; equality passes. Q4 compares A3 and A1 regret and counts controllers whose selected action mean success is ≥10pp below F10. T2/T3 repeat all available metrics descriptively. Label every conclusion DEVELOPMENT. T1 is an external zero-shot test of a frozen model, not a novel prospective confirmation, because its labels were opened in round14A.

No decision threshold, action menu, architecture, hyperparameter, metric, or inferential rule will change after opening external labels. Any implementation correction after freeze must be documented with a new receipt and cannot use external labels to choose a model or rule.
