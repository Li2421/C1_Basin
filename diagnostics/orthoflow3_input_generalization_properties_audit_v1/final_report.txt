Success Basin C1: input properties and why state-aware LOSO underperforms eta-only

Conclusion
----------
The current input has useful cross-scene structural properties, but it is not
established as a sufficient statistic for closed-loop feasibility or as an
identifiable target mapping from the three source scenes. These are separate
requirements. Current failure does not prove that a controller-conditioned Q
can never generalize.

This audit used existing frozen checkpoints, candidate pools and compatible
outcome evidence. It ran 24 frozen model replays and diagnostic branch/input
substitutions, with zero new training and NEW ROLLOUT=0. All 24 replays reproduced
saved logits exactly. No production model, normalization, label, or historical
LOSO result was changed. Target labels had already been opened: all new
substitutions below are post-hoc mechanism tests, not new confirmation scores.

What would a generalizable input need?
------------------------------------
Let w denote the full physical state, environment, controller, monitor memory,
horizon, and stochastic continuation semantics. Let z=phi(w) be the supplied
physical entities and controller summary. A representation sufficient for Q
would satisfy:

  phi(w1)=phi(w2) => Q(w1,eta)=Q(w2,eta)

over the intended admissible controllers and scenes. Equivalently, scene or
controller identity should supply no additional success information once
z and eta are known. This is a property of the represented physical system,
not of tensor shapes or neural network capacity.

Learning that map from three source scenes also requires evidence or justified
physical restrictions connecting source inputs to target inputs. Overlapping
support is a sufficient route, not a universal mathematical necessity: an
appropriate mechanistic model can extrapolate. The current generic learned
MLP, however, is not supplied with such a transfer guarantee.

A concrete non-identification example occurs in the Ring fold. Curvature and
the curved-obstacle indicator are identically zero in all source obstacle
entities. For any candidate logit function f(z,eta), adding a term that vanishes
when curvature=0 but changes eta ordering on curved geometry leaves every
source prediction/loss unchanged. Source TRAIN/VAL alone cannot identify which
extension is correct on Ring. More expressive networks do not remove this
ambiguity. They require additional data or defensible structural restrictions.

Properties already established
------------------------------
* Shared physical schema, variable entity masks and counts, shared entity
  encoders, goal-relative vectors, time/monitor fields, and common eta basis.
* Archived permutation/passive-coordinate/mixed-padding checks pass. The
  Toy integration basis-equivalence error was zero; mixed-padding error was
  7.45e-9. Native CPU/GPU checks passed. These are useful symmetry/interface
  properties, not evidence of invariance across different future controllers.
* New replay of every H8 structured/contrast checkpoint, three seeds by four
  folds, matched stored target logits with maximum error zero.
* Current normalization uses fixed physical units and source-only eta/context
  statistics. There is no target-trained normalization or explicit scene ID.

Properties missing or unestablished
----------------------------------
1. Original (h,eta) sufficiency has strong direct counterevidence: a matched
   future-controller swap with h error 5.97e-16 caused eight of 64 pairs to go
   from 16/16 to 0/16; nine comparisons passed the recorded Holm-adjusted
   significance check. This establishes an omitted-controller problem for
   that admissible controller family, not that it alone causes every LOSO loss.
2. Sufficiency of richer C remains UNDERRESOLVED. H8/H20 summarize only short
   trajectories, often by means/extrema/integrals; probe stochastic dispersion
   is saved but not given to the H8 critic. Nominal context does not encode the
   candidate's future response. Even eta-conditioned finite probes do not in
   general specify controller behavior after leaving the probed states.
   Thirty Ring matched comparisons have |delta Q16|>=.75; 24 are close under
   the chosen H20 metric, 23 under H100 nominal, 22 under the short function
   signature. These are poor smoothness/metric diagnostics. They are NOT exact
   augmented-input collisions and do NOT prove that every function of C is
   non-identifiable. A small distance or low distance/Q correlation alone is
   insufficient for that conclusion.
3. Source coverage is inadequate for identifying Ring extrapolation. Current
   source TRAIN contains no curved obstacles. The raw target curvature channel
   ranges from -1.23077 to 4.44444 after fixed unit scaling, versus exactly zero
   in source. The curved flag is 1 versus source 0; policy-axis-required is 0
   versus source 1. The two zero source channels give their first Dense kernel
   rows zero data gradient (apart from regularizer effects), so their target
   effect was never learned from curvature variation. Source controller H8
   support also misses Ring: every one of 60 targets is outside the source
   1--99% envelope on at least three channels (median five).
4. No explicit scene ID does not ensure the absence of a scene shortcut. Agent
   count, obstacle count/curvature, speed, horizon, chart/monitor flags almost
   identify these fixed scenes. These quantities are physically legitimate;
   removing them indiscriminately would also remove relevant information. The
   problem is a lack of varied physical/controller regimes breaking their
   correlations with eta preference. Matched Flow interventions provide some
   such variation, but only in a small set of closely related controllers and
   source families. They do not create Ring-like source geometry in the Ring
   LOSO fold.
5. The scene encoder pools learned messages to 128 dimensions. Permutation
   compatibility and no observed exact embedding aliases do not establish
   injectivity or retention of all long-horizon-relevant interaction structure.

New functional evidence: where wrong rankings are created
---------------------------------------------------------
The frozen structured model is z=B(eta,C)+I(h,eta,C), where z is a logit. Removing
one branch at inference identifies its effect inside this fitted network.
Branches were jointly trained and are not uniquely identifiable causal
components: I also sees eta/C and need not be a pure state residual; B includes
state-derived context. B-alone is not the separately trained eta-only model.

Toy, same target K16 pools, independent training seeds 17/23/41:

                         Full        B(eta,C) alone
  structured_full        34,31,33     41,40,41 /48
  contrast_full          33,31,34     40,41,42 /48
  frozen eta-only                    42/48 source-selected

All six networks improve when the branch containing full state interaction is
removed. This directly supports harmful learned extrapolated ranking in this
implementation, not a claim that physical state is intrinsically harmful.
Toy was already known to have real fixed-eta state dependence: 49/122
informative eta pairs reversed order across 32 held-out states, and a fixed-eta
state model improved MAE .357 -> .080. The signal exists inside Toy; the learned
three-scene interaction does not transfer it.

Ring is different. The source-selected structured checkpoint is seed23:

  full:                  0/60, selected mean logit 24.249
  prior B alone:         0/60, selected mean logit 19.270
  interaction I alone:   0/60
  separate eta-only:    27/60

At the full model's chosen proposals, mean prior contribution is +19.136 and
mean interaction contribution is +5.113. Predicted p is effectively one, while
the cached empirical Q16 interval averages .0094--.0302. Thus Ring is not
explained solely by an interaction branch corrupting an otherwise correct
prior. The learned context-dependent prior already makes the wrong ordering.
Prior-only Ring counts for structured seeds are 0,0,12; contrast seeds 0,0,0.

Two diagnostic input substitutions sharpen the interpretation:
* Resetting Ring-only curvature/curved channels to zero, or resetting all raw
  source-constant novel channels to source values, leaves the selected model
  at 0/60. The unsupported curvature channels are real, but their direct random
  contribution is not a sufficient explanation of the failure.
* Replacing C by the source TRAIN mean still gives the full structured models
  0,0,4/60. Prior-only under that substitution gives 0,4,42/60. The large
  seed dependence precludes treating this as a repair. It shows that learned
  prior/context routing and interactions matter, not one faulty input field.

These substitutions can violate physical input consistency and are not
deployment proposals. No target outcome selected a new checkpoint or model.

Why eta-only can be better
-------------------------
Eta-only estimates source-marginal eta preference. The state-aware model can
reduce source likelihood loss by conditioning on geometry/controller variables
that track scene and eta outcomes. That conditional advantage only transfers
if the learned conditional relation remains valid in the target regime.

Archived Ring-fold source VAL shared NLL .262/.094/.036 (Toy/DB/Four) improves
over eta-only .549/.348/.374, so the state branch has not simply failed to fit
source labels. On unseen Ring the conditional extension is wrong. Eta-only
cannot take that particular high-dimensional extrapolation path, which makes
it the stronger current lower-complexity baseline, although 27/60 is still far
from oracle 60/60. It has no universal lower-bound guarantee.

The old physical-support audit is consistent: target Ring median state distance
10.07 versus source leave-family p95 .691, while eta distance is only .149.
Those values depend on the registered metric and are support diagnostics, not
a theorem that distance causes Q errors.

Source early-stop negative omission was a separate real bug. After repair,
source Four negative mean prediction fell .9157 -> .0274 and source NLL improved,
but Ring stayed 0/60. Missing negatives alone is therefore ruled out as a
sufficient remaining cause. Joint supervised Ring58/60 and target-label
adaptation establish fitting capacity when target support is supplied; they
do not validate a transferable invariant. Two Ring TRAIN families gave
eta-only47/60 on all three seeds versus adapted state-aware44/47/47. This is
post-hoc evidence for a strong scene-specific eta prior, not zero-shot evidence.

Interpretation of confidence and input use
-----------------------------------------
A sigmoid near one is a fitted probability, not a measurement of epistemic
certainty. Source NLL does not calibrate arbitrary target inputs. Selecting
the maximum among 16 proposals exposes the largest optimistic errors.
Within-Ring state/context shuffle being ineffective also does not prove the
network ignores every such input: all replacements share much of Ring's
scene-level information. Branch/context substitutions demonstrate stronger
effects when moving across regimes. Input-use claims must distinguish use as
a scene/difficulty cue from correctly predicted conditional eta preference.

Root-cause adjudication
-----------------------
HIGH: Source physical/controller support and conditional-shift limitation.
  For: absent curvature supervision, disjoint physical neighborhoods, very
  strong recovery from a few target families, source vs target fit contrast.
  Against/limit: no matched source-support-expansion intervention yet proves
  this alone repairs strict LOSO; eta-only also remains imperfect.
HIGH (fitted-network behavior): Non-transferring conditional eta ordering.
  For: six Toy branch removals improve, both Ring branches fail, source fit is
  strong, Ring high-probability picks fail, no stable controller-reversal use.
  Against/limit: functional ablations do not uniquely assign causal blame to h
  or prove a particular optimizer/architecture is the unique cause.
HIGH for original h; UNDERRESOLVED for richer C: Missing long-horizon controller
  information / summary sufficiency.
  For: matched exact-h future-controller intervention; compact probes omit
  unvisited future behavior and lose temporal/entity detail.
  Against/limit: no exact identical augmented (h,eta,C) with a certified
  different Q has been demonstrated; richer C failure may still be learning.

Not supported: theoretical impossibility of all cross-scene Q; no state signal;
generator coverage as the cause of these perfect-oracle-pool selection losses;
a newly discovered train/inference normalization or sigmoid-tie bug. The
current audit reproduced all24 frozen logits exactly, and historical interface
tests passed. This does not claim exhaustive verification of every simulator
component.

Two decisive remaining experiments, not launched in this audit
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. A source-only held-controller-out matched design with the same eta probes
   across independent source families and controllers. Test whether a frozen C
   predicts Q/ranking changes when controller identity is held out, using
   eta-only/additive controls. Failure here precedes any credible LOSO claim.
   Existing matched diagnostics show this gate is not yet passed; repeating
   the same narrow checkpoint variants without new information is low value.
2. A predeclared source physical-support intervention (e.g. curvature and
   controller response varied independently within TRAIN), keeping critic/C
   fixed, followed by a new untouched target confirmation set. Improvement
   would support coverage; persistent failure despite covered response regimes
   would increase concern about C sufficiency/learning. No target TEST tuning.
