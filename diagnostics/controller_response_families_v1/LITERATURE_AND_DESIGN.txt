Stage: controller response diversity, authorized 2026-10-09.
Primary literature consulted before constructing this stage:
 - PD-VF: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/raileanu20a.html
   Separates policy behavior and dynamics, crosses controllers/environments.
 - PeVFA: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09536
   Policy behavior reconstruction tests representation independently of value.
 - PVN: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11833
   Action fingerprints are a practical representation, not a general theorem
   that nominal endpoint actions determine intervened FF trajectories.
 - Rectified Flow: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03003
   Treats trajectory/vector-field geometry as a distinct transport property.
   This paper does not validate our controllers or prove our Q transfer claim.

Our mechanism control (not claimed to reproduce a published algorithm):
Stable affine fields dh/dtau = A(b-h), with positive symmetric part and a
contractive ten-step Euler map. Different A matrices give isotropic, directional,
rotating and inter-agent-coupled responses. Select b analytically so the
unprojected nominal endpoint is the SAME cheap physical feedback action for
every controller, state and random seed. Eta is still injected by the existing
FF operator, with its original safety projection. The unprojected eta response
operator is A^-1 [I-(I-A/10)^10], which can change direction and coupling.
This isolates information absent from a nominal-action fingerprint. Projected
closed-loop Q may or may not differ; real rollouts, not this algebra, decide.

Small DEVELOPMENT test, no eta search:
 - Reuse first8 pre-existing TRAIN physical states per scene (Toy/Ring).
 - Exactly four pre-fixed candidates from the unchanged K16 pool: 0,5,10,15.
 - Two fixed rollout seeds:0,1, shared across all controllers/candidates.
 - Five cheap endpoint-matched families plus the exact native Flow reference.
 - 768 requested attempts maximum; exact cache preflight before simulation.
 - No critic training, eta optimization, family selection from success labels,
   final-confirmation states, or broad eta sweep in this stage.
 - Static input/FF-action checks first; all five valid families retained even
   when their responses or success outcomes are uninteresting.
 - All states/candidates/controllers/seeds frozen before inspecting new labels.
 - Q2/8-state results are mechanism probes, not robust B15 or transfer claims.

The earlier frozen 34816-attempt confirmation continues unchanged. Its data,
models and evaluation definitions are not retrofitted to this new mechanism.
