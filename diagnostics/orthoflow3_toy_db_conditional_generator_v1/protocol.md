# Frozen protocol

The selector, canonical mode IDs, Toy anchors, transformed DB anchors, source splits, and normalizations are frozen. Positive evidence is empirical Q>=0.90; negative evidence is Q<=0.50. Eta is assigned only to its nearest scenario anchor within normalized radius 0.25. The generator is a shared diagonal tanh-squashed Gaussian with scenario adapters. TEST outcomes are quarantined until checkpoints and VAL choice are frozen.
