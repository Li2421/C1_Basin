Per-state success geometry is informative but uneven. Toy generator TRAIN has median 7 B15 eta/state, and 40/128 states have a known positive–negative pair within 0.10 normalized eta distance. The critic has median 3 B15 pairs/state and near-boundary positive–negative evidence on 18/48 states. These numbers show that Toy contains genuine set-valued eta evidence, while DB generator training mostly teaches a set of broadly successful old anchors, not local continuous boundary geometry. Multiple separated positive samples alone do not certify disconnected basins or a need for multimodality.

## B. Split and leakage verdict

The generator Toy, generator DB, and critic Toy TRAIN/VAL/TEST sets have zero exact state UID overlap, zero source-group overlap, and zero identical state–eta pair overlap. The Toy 200-state hard cohort has zero state UID and source-group overlap with both current training sets; its nearest generator TRAIN h has standardized 214-D RMS distance at least 0.072 (none below 0.05). Within the frozen splits, nearest TEST-to-TRAIN h RMS distances are at least 0.144 for generator Toy, 0.677 for generator DB, and 0.228 for critic Toy. The critic's TEST eta are also spatially separated from the combined TRAIN eta, minimum normalized distance 0.084. The relevant inventories are true-t0 records, not randomly split adjacent rollout timesteps.

**Verdict: `STATE_SPLIT_CLEAN`; no evidence of `HARD_COHORT_CONTAMINATED` or adjacent-trajectory leakage.** Source-group naming is not a proof of perfect statistical independence across every physical family, but exact identities and feature-neighbor checks reveal no obvious leak. The generator loader deliberately uses all eta on TRAIN states rather than honoring the older eta-holdout flag; hence this generator was evaluated for *unseen-state*, not unseen-eta, generalization. Do not relabel that result as an unseen-eta experiment.

## C. DB: what data are actually missing?

On the DB generator's 64 TRAIN states, the frozen best fixed transformed eta is B15 on **64/64**. Among 12 tested anchors, each state has 9 or 10 B15 anchors; there is no observed fixed-fail/adaptive-success or narrow-panel state. Safety has a complete standard-16 record for only 8 of these 64: 7 are Safety B15 (`EASY` under the requested definition), 1 is `CORRECTION_NEEDED`, and 56 cannot be classified by Safety from current compatible seed evidence. All 64 are at least fixed-easy; no `FIXED_FAIL_ADAPTIVE_SUCCESS` or `NO_KNOWN_BASIN` is observed.

The broader original DB controller profile has 132 source groups with complete standard-16 fixed-eta outcomes: **132/132 fixed B15**. Safety has complete standard-16 evidence for 76: 20 B15 and 56 non-B15 with robust correction available. This distinguishes genuine correction-needed states from fixed-fail exception states. The separately frozen 48-state outcome-blind DB hard cohort uses a different, internally consistent controller/RNG fingerprint. On its own standard 16 seeds, fixed is B15 on 48/48; Safety is B15 on 7/48 and non-B15 on 41/48. Its previous full Q64 evaluation gave Safety B63 0/48 and fixed B63 48/48. Crucially, **the current mode-free generator and current W1 critic were never closed-loop tested on those 48 states**. The old *mode-conditioned* generator's DB 16/16 result cannot substitute for this test.

Thus DB is **not short of rollout rows in general**: it has many correction-needed states and strong fixed-controller evidence. The missing evidence is specifically (i) current generator performance on held-out correction-needed states, (ii) any demonstrated fixed-fail/adaptive-success or narrow Basin state under the frozen controller, and (iii) DB TRAIN off-anchor/boundary examples. The phrase `DB_HARD_STATE_COVERAGE_INSUFFICIENT` is warranted for claims about *adaptive continuous generation*, not for claims that the fixed correction works. Existing tested DB states may simply lack the regime where a richer generator would matter; absence of observed exceptions is not proof none exist.

For the minimal necessary test, the current frozen generator mean was computed on the existing 48-state DB hard manifest using its preserved 80-D true-t0 features. The global DB preflight requests 48×16 = **768** matched continuations: exact reuse 0, partial reuse 0, aggregate reuse 0, truly missing 768. **Nothing was executed.** The planner's `incompatible=768` counter is a generic no-record classification in its implementation, not a demonstrated semantic mismatch: the hard-panel controller UID has `EXACT_PROFILE`, the authoritative OrthoFlow3 hash, and true-t0 latched semantics. This preflight uses the hard panel's own RNG/controller fingerprint, not the earlier DB split fingerprint. Files: `db_hard_frozen_generator_mean.json`, `db_hard_mean_planned_rollouts.json`, `db_hard_mean_cache_preflight.json`.

## D. Current critic versus generator proposals

The W1 critic's 2,876 Toy TRAIN pairs cover 549 distinct eta; 1,920 are shared matrix probes and 956 are historical wide probes. Its empirical Q≥0.9 fraction is 48.5%, versus 67.1% B15 among the 3,200 frozen K=16 deployment proposals. Across 3D domain-normalized eta coordinates, 75.5% of generated proposals are farther than 0.10 from the nearest critic TRAIN eta, and 24.0% farther than 0.20. This is a real **proposal-distribution mismatch**, even though the crude intermediate-Q fraction is similar (TRAIN 7.0%, proposals 6.5%). The critic has no training pairs from the actual 200 hard states, appropriately preserving their test isolation.

The 13 K=16 states with a B15 proposal but non-B15 critic choice are listed in `critic_13_failure_states.csv`. Their critic-selected true Q16 averages 0.567 while the oracle proposal averages 0.990; the selected proposal's median predicted success probability is 0.973. However the successful oracle proposal's nearest-TRAIN-eta distance median is 0.134, versus 0.145 for *all* B15 proposals. Thus these 13 errors are **not concentrated in the most spatially unseen eta**. State-conditioned misranking/overconfidence near sharp feasibility changes is at least as plausible as simple coordinate OOD. This audit establishes `CRITIC_PROPOSAL_DISTRIBUTION_SHIFT`, but does not prove it alone causes all 13 mistakes. Deployment probability bins are in `critic_deployment_calibration.csv`.

## E. Necessary evidence, without starting a new experiment

The 20-item Toy/DB/other-scene status matrix is `necessary_tests.csv`; it points to actual artifacts rather than report titles. In brief:

- **MUST DO before a two-scenario paper claim:** evaluate the frozen current mode-free generator mean on the source-isolated DB hard panel against cached Safety/fixed (the 768-missing preflight above); only if it adds value, run the DB finite-proposal critic comparison under a frozen plan. A claim that state adaptation beats a fixed eta additionally needs a discriminative fixed-fail/adaptive-success panel, which the present compatible DB inventory does not contain. Confirm any finally selected controller with independent Q64 and collision/hard-safety reporting.
- **SHOULD DO:** complete the offline Toy 13-state critic misranking diagnosis, quantify DB local boundary/narrow-basin evidence under a future frozen data plan, and do DB K-sweep/latency only when the frozen mean test makes that expenditure informative.
- **OPTIONAL:** K=32 Toy and other-scene pipeline evaluation. Neither is a prerequisite for this dataset audit, and neither was launched.

No new generator, mixture, flow, critic architecture, training run, or rollout was initiated.
