These execution semantics cannot be merged by matching a name alone.

**Directly computable Toy metrics:** source row, actual state, η, raw and executed yield/pass coordinates, CBF slacks, stage membership, `d_c` and hypothetical raw `Bη†` from saved anchor/B. `EXPLOITABILITY_METRICS.csv` records these. **Not directly computable from saved actions:** `r_m` without a predeclared stage action-row specification and projection of `u0`, the actual TT `η†` action, continuous-box feasibility, full η† trajectory. The audited 68-action panel has zero eligible rows in the archived `K_entry/K_pass`; it therefore yields no coverage estimate.

**Ring prerequisite:** establish a Ring stage/action structure using its polygon, six pair constraints, and actual goal coordination. `THEOREM_AUDIT.md` gives one narrow reference-rotation structure. Necessary, phase-independent tests on the 24 new test initial states exclude all 24 from that precise open class (`RING_INITIAL_COVERAGE.json`); this does not exclude later trajectory entry or a broader structure. Compatible B/anchor reconstruction, η† action margins and temporal continuation remain unmeasured. Reconstructing controller actions may be possible from saved checkpoints/parameters and original keys, but exact byte replay must first be verified on a held trace; no such reconstruction was run here.

Count denominators separately: the archived Toy panel has 68 actions, 17 selected state/key entries, one controller instance, and no eligible stage actions; it is not 68 independent episodes. Round26/Round29/cross-type rates use their original controller/state/seed units and clustered intervals. This audit does not turn those endpoint labels into a new independent sample or compute a misleading cross-Controller exploitability CI.
