# 论文实验设置—基线

本轮比较通过固定作者源码原环境复现的 Zhang等（T-RO 2025）死锁感知控制（DAC）和 Lee等（IROS 2025）Merry-Go-Round（MGR）。DAC作者随机障碍演示20/20、MGR作者矩形场地演示60/60到达，独立连续段几何检查保留正安全裕量；这些是单个演示复现，并非论文多种子表格均值的复现。Chen等（RA-L 2023）的默认原生H形演示虽采样帧安全且到达，但按统一连续几何复核存在帧间重叠，按预登记准入规则退出；该结论不否定其采样约束理论。Wang2017一致扰动及完整死锁消解、Zhang ICRA2025自适应风险方法因没有可核验作者代码和参数退出。本轮端口只撤掉作者原实现没有的返回码拦截，保留作者算法、求解器、迭代上限与容差，并按共同物理尺寸、安全距离及0.08m到达条件适配；原文安全距离另作参照。所有参数仅在训练场景上用同一两轮规则筛选：16组在状态0–1运行，按精确随机子集最优期望增益达到3个百分点时一次扩到32组，排名前4组补状态2–7。冻结参数、划分和分析计划后才运行正式测试。基准甲为24个独立测试场景、每场景16个初态，6个既有学习型名义控制器在物理初态内平均；8个验证场景只作描述。方形与十字场景均使用预登记合法性门通过的物理一致版本，学习模型不重训练；旧版本标签单独保留。基准乙为17类代表场景的新状态12–19，双方固定参数对比；我们主族与原四列族各保留两个MPC名义控制器。主终点按物理初态配对、类别内场景及场景内状态分层重抽10000次，场景层双侧符号翻转100000次，Holm家族保留三个作者代码候选，退出方法不伪造p值。成功条件均时、共同成功配对时间、全队列截断时间和单步计算时间分别报告，缺失时间不补零；缺失结果另给按失败计的敏感性。可选Oracle仅在全部固定策略主线之后按冻结前16组直接搜索参数、首次成功即停，表示有限候选池覆盖，不训练或调用Generator。局限包括代表场景参与过机制设计、验证集已用于原方法设计、旧模型训练于旧墙体模型，以及端口与原生论文环境的信息和动力学差异。

We compare the author-code DAC (Zhang et al., T-RO 2025) and MGR (Lee et al., IROS 2025) ports after reproducing unchanged native demonstrations with all robots reaching their goals and positive independently checked continuous-segment safety margins. Native reproduction here concerns individual demonstrations rather than published multi-seed averages. Chen et al. (RA-L 2023) is excluded by the prespecified native safety gate because its default H-shaped demonstration has intersample overlap despite sampled-state safety and arrival; this does not refute its discrete constraint guarantees. Both Wang2017 variants and the ZhangICRA2025 adaptive-risk port are excluded for lack of verifiable author code and parameters. Adapters remove only extra return-status rejection, retain the authors’ solvers, iteration limits, tolerances and mechanisms, and align physical clearances and the common0.08m arrival criterion; original clearances are reported separately. Symmetric training-only selection evaluates16 settings on states0–1, expands once to32 when the exact expected-best subset gain meets3 percentage points, and evaluates only the top4 settings on states2–7. Parameters, splits and the analysis plan are frozen before formal testing. BenchmarkA uses24 held-out scenes with16 physical initial states per scene; six frozen learned nominal models remain one paired block. Eight validation scenes are descriptive. Physically consistent box/intersection variants passed the predefined initial-validity gate; learned models remain trained on the original geometry. BenchmarkB uses17 representative scene types and newly generated states12–19, with two frozen MPC nominals for each C1 correction family. The primary endpoint uses10000 hierarchical bootstrap replicates and100000 scene-level sign flips, with a three-method preregistered Holm family and no fabricated tests for excluded methods. Successful completion time, matched-success time, horizon-capped time and per-step compute are reported separately, with explicit missingness and a missing-as-failure sensitivity. Optional finite-pool Oracle@16 is run only after fixed-policy comparisons, searching frozen parameter settings directly and stopping at first success, without generator training or generation. Limitations include scene-design leakage on BenchmarkB, previously used validation scenes, the geometry shift without retraining, and documented differences between common-plant ports and native paper environments.
