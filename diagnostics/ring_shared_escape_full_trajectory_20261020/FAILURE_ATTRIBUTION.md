# Failure attribution boundaries

The archived episodes have no states in the old strict Ring domain. The reason is **identified exactly**: its fixed-goal radius tolerance conflicts with every one of the **104 distinct test states** across the four panels. This is a **FAILED distribution-coverage claim** for that certificate, not a geometry impossibility theorem. The actual Ring polygon/pair QP remains feasible for the new candidate's reference actions; 24 cross-type 1c and 48 cross-type 3 new starts have strictly positive 390-row margins in both circulation signs.

| Possible mechanism | Evidence grade | What the archive permits |
|---|---|---|
| Shared safe stage itself infeasible | **FAILED** for the broad candidate's stated initial class | Analytic reference and 24 finite 390-row checks give a nonempty safe set. Feasibility at later arbitrary conflict states remains **UNKNOWN**. |
| Old strict reference domain covers historical trajectories | **FAILED** | Static goals exclude every saved step of 73,358 episodes. This does not test other Ring domains. |

Selected TT outputs sometimes enter the exploratory `E`, proving existence of an effective selected η at those particular states, but finite menu failures at other states and the continuous box are untested. |

| Selected parameter fails while another frozen menu member succeeds | **UNKNOWN** | Other arms were evaluated from the same *initial* state but follow different physical trajectories. Their actions are not counterfactual projections at a selected mid-trajectory state/key. The saved menu cannot be treated as a per-state action oracle without exact replay. |
| Critic/controller conditioning explains success | **UNKNOWN** | Historical cross-type 1c reported conditioned minus blind `−1.00 pp [−3.22,+1.35]` on its own paired protocol. The current geometric counts do not identify selector causality, and Flow uses FF. |

The related common-direction angular exit is much more frequent. |
| One positive action guarantees a full angular stage or task success | **FAILED** as an inference | Candidate action membership is often isolated; an exit and final success have independent geometric/event requirements. `longest_candidate_run` and `first_angular_exit_step` are separate columns. |

The candidate geometry admits safe coordinated circulation; actual trajectories can reorganize through asynchronous yielding, radial detours and Controller-dependent timing. The current scan does not establish which of those explains the successful episodes. It also does not show that the original B basis fails: to identify expression, anchor suppression or selection requires a matched state/key/controller projection audit. Numerically abnormal episodes retain their original `numerical_failure` label; timeout is not recoded as deadlock. No inference uses success to define the old or exploratory stage.
