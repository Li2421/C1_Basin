Question: why can a critic fail to learn a transferable S x C x eta relation?
Separate identification (input sufficiency), informative joint support,
estimation/noise, approximation/optimization, training objective, deployment
shift, and implementation/evidence integrity. Fitting an input-output relation
on source data does not by itself identify the held-scene extension.

   cache improves independent controller-effect predictions. It is a real bug,
   not a complete explanation of all four-scene transfer failures.
   Evidence: ../controller_cached_ff_relearn_v1/.
