## Frozen labels and analysis

For controller and arm, lower rate = successes / (4×32), upper = (successes+unknown)/(4×32) within complete waves, with each controller always evaluated on 32 states and seeds 0–3. E=S500−F10, L=S500−F13, N=F13−F10=E−L using lower rates; report corresponding interval arithmetic bounds. t* is highest-rate schedule arm, ties to later t. a* is highest-rate of eight arms, ties by stated arm order F10,F13,F2,S200,S300,S400,S500,S600. Split-half reliability compares seeds 0–1 with 2–3 for every arm, E,L,N,t*,a*: report correlation where defined and agreement for discrete labels, with uncertainty and undefined cases explicit. Unknown stays missing for the upper-bound sensitivity.

Variance decomposition: one-way random-effects ICC by recipe for N,E,L,t*, between-recipe and within-recipe seed variance fractions, 95% cluster bootstrap CI by resampling 14 recipes with replacement (10,000 draws, RNG seed 20261014). Report number of recipes with discordant N signs among four seeds; prediction at least 3/14 is descriptive, not a gate.

Three representations: R5-PCA2, R6-Delta, concatenation. Source-only column mean imputation, z-score, and PCA are refit within each training set. Ridge alpha=1, intercept unpenalized, no tuning. New-recipe CV: seven fixed folds, two recipes per fold in lexical recipe order (consecutive pairs); train on other 12 recipes with first k seed offsets for k=1,2,3,4; test all four seeds of held-out recipes. New-seed CV: hold out each offset s=1..4 across 14 recipes, train on first k of remaining offsets in ascending order, k=1,2,3. Report per-fold and aggregate metrics.

For E,L,N report Spearman (undefined if constant), three-way sign accuracy (negative, zero, positive), RMSE and ratio to the within-fold source-mean RMSE. For t*, report fraction within ±100 steps.
