Illustrative example selected from the prespecified 32 TEST states: Toy GiveWay, tt_ff_v1_toy_give_way_test_945530001. TT and Field are compared at the same state using the same 16 prespecified three-dimensional Sobol interventions and the same 16 continuation seeds per intervention. Membership uses the B15 robust criterion: at least 15 of 16 continuations succeed. Gray circles are failures for both controllers; blue downward triangles are TT-only robust interventions (breaks); orange upward triangles are Field-only robust interventions (rescues); green squares are robust for both (shared membership). The translucent TT and Field convex hulls use their respective sampled robust interventions, including shared points. Hulls are visual envelopes of sampled robust interventions, not certified continuous basin boundaries. They do not estimate an exact basin volume, boundary, or topology. The samples are empirical points; no additional eta coordinates or interpolated memberships are shown.
