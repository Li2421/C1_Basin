Pairwise illustrative views of the four frozen factorial formulations in the two physical scenarios represented in the archived data: Toy GiveWay and Ring Exchange. Each A/B comparison uses one selected state per scenario and the same 16 prespecified 3D Sobol interventions at that state, with the same 16 continuation seeds per intervention. Membership is B15 (at least 15 successful continuations of 16); unresolved B15 cells are not treated as failures and were excluded from candidate state selection. Gray circles are robust for neither formulation, blue downward triangles are A-only, orange upward triangles are B-only, and green squares are robust for both. TT/TF/FT/FF encode safety location in the first letter (T terminal, F field) and correction location in the second (T terminal, F within Flow); FT includes the protocol's mandatory terminal safety closure. States are illustrative and selected independently for each scene and pair. Candidates rank by membership changes, then the smaller of the two one-sided category counts, then the smaller robust count, then state ID. The preferred thresholds are at least five robust points per formulation and at least four changed memberships; if needed they relax through 4/4/3, 3/3/2, 2/2/1, and 1/1/0 (A robust/B robust/change). At each level, the next ranked candidates among the top five are checked for two full-rank hulls. If no dual hull is available, only empirical points are shown. The exact selected thresholds and counts are in factorial_pair_selections.csv; top-five counts are in factorial_pair_candidates.csv. Convex hulls, when present, are visual envelopes of sampled robust interventions, not certified continuous basin boundaries; they do not establish exact volume, boundary, or topology. No new eta coordinates, interpolated membership, rollout, or training was used.
