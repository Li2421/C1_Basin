The first letter denotes safety location, the second correction location.
TT = terminal safety and terminal correction: P_U(P_U(native_Flow(x,xi))+g_eta).
FF = field safety and field correction. At a fixed physical state, let h denote
the physical action-space Flow iterate (not the state-conditioning feature h
of earlier reports), s the pseudo-time and delta the anchored physical correction.
The proposed vector field is u_theta(x,h,s)+delta. Its admissible projection is
  w = [P_U(h + (1-s)(u_theta+delta)) - h] / (1-s).
Each Euler update h += ds*w is implemented as a convex interpolation toward the
projected point. At the final step it reaches the certified action set. FF applies
no additional terminal correction or clip, only a hard-safety assertion.

TF integrates correction within the Flow, without field safety, and projects the
terminal result into the same hard-safety set. FT integrates field safety with
zero correction, then adds delta and applies the mandatory terminal closure.
The basis is OrthoFlow3: goal, Flow-perpendicular, relation. Every formulation
recomputes the same basis rule from its own physical state and native safe Flow
reference each physical step. At the same physical state and seed the anchored
delta agrees; after trajectories diverge, state-dependent values appropriately
diverge. Eta is held across the episode; the physical lifting is held through
one pseudo-time integration. No extra gain rescaling, training or proposal
selection is introduced by this analysis.

TT->FF therefore measures the full formulation change. It does not alone isolate
correction relocation from safety relocation. The existing TT->TF and FT->FF
factorial contrasts supply that control. FT's terminal closure and TT's native
decoder/clipping prevent a claim of perfect abstract factor orthogonality.
Eta-zero matched controls and archived integration refinement constrain these
issues; they do not prove that all implementation mechanisms are eliminated.

6. Inference and remaining scope

State/source family is the independent inference unit. Bootstrap resamples
whole states and keeps all eta/seed dependence intact; scene-stratified pooled
resampling keeps the50/50 Toy/Ring target mixture. The primary exact sign test
uses per-state net robust-count changes, with ties reported. The supplemental
whole-state sign-flip test assumes sign exchangeability/symmetry; it is not a
random-treatment-assignment test. Neither a pooled512-cell McNemar test nor
rollout-level binomial standard errors are used for the main effect.

Separate exact matched-seed tests, Holm-corrected across512 cells, support actual
outcome changes beyond anecdotal threshold crossings. They condition on common
scientifically valid slots and do not establish general missingness assumptions.
Seed resampling is a descriptive stability check conditional on the same finite
seeds; it cannot certify a latent population success probability. Thresholds14,
15 and16 are all shown, without selecting the most favorable definition.

The report's strongest allowed decision denotes net empirical expansion, not
set inclusion:16 primary breaks and two states with volume decreases are real.
There is no connectedness, topology, hole, safety interpolation or continuous
Lebesgue-volume claim. A broader claim over the continuous box would require
new independently scrambled eta banks and corresponding new paired labels;
those are unnecessary for the explicitly empirical target in this request.
